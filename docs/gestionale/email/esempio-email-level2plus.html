What we will work on

The workshop focuses on using the technical foundations already acquired in Level 1 and Level 2 to create different shapes, patterns, transitions and tying possibilities.

  • variations based on Level 1 and Level 2 material;
  • different shapes and patterns within Kinbaku LuXuria style;
  • exercises inspired by “The Iconic 6”;
  • body lacing structures;
  • additional ties and exercises usually not included in Level 1 and Level 2;
  • longer transition sequences;
  • combination of known elements into more articulated structures;
  • aesthetic and technical refinement;
  • adaptation of the tie to the body and to the situation;
  • development of a more personal and flexible use of the style.
◆ Prerequisites

For riggers:

  • attendance at Level 1 and Level 2;
  • solid Gote;
  • confidence with the fundamental elements of Kinbaku LuXuria style;
  • safe and efficient management of previous-level material;
  • good rope handling skills;
  • readiness to work on longer sequences, transitions and variations.

For models:

  • familiarity with Level 1 and Level 2 positions and shapes;
  • comfort with the intensity and demands of Kinbaku LuXuria style;
  • clear communication of sensations, limits and needs;
  • readiness for more complex structures and longer transition sequences.

Switching roles with your partner will not be possible during the workshop.
What to bring

Ropes: please bring a full rope set suitable for Kinbaku LuXuria practice. Unless otherwise specified by the teachers, we recommend at least 12 ropes, preferably jute, approximately 7–7.5 metres long and 5–6 mm in diameter.

Synthetic ropes are not allowed. Cotton, linen and other materials not suitable for this type of practice are also not recommended.

Clothing: comfortable, fitted or sporty clothing is recommended. Models should choose clothing suitable for rope practice, allowing freedom of movement and avoiding anything that may get caught in the ropes. Since the workshop may include aesthetic work, shapes and presentation, you may also bring clothing or elements that fit the atmosphere you want to explore.
